# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Everyone here is doing this in their spare
time, for free, and nobody owes you a response.

## What is expected

- Treat disagreement as a technical problem, not a personal one.
- Accept that "no" is a complete answer to a feature request, and that maintainers
  get to decide what this project is.
- Remember that most people filing issues here are creators, not engineers. A
  question that seems obvious to you took them an hour to work up to asking. Answer
  it, or say nothing.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, unwelcome sexual attention,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of a thread.
None of that is tolerated anywhere in this project — issues, pull requests,
discussions, or commit messages.

## Reporting

Email `charanxmn@gmail.com`. Reports are handled privately. You will not be asked
to justify why something bothered you.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close threads, and block accounts, at their
discretion and without a warning first. There is no appeals process. This is a small
project and the bar for removal is low, deliberately — the cost of losing one
difficult contributor is much lower than the cost of losing everyone else.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, and to any public conduct that would make
someone reasonably wonder whether this is a safe place to file an issue.
